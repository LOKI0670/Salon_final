Our Story

The Atelier

Founded 2009

Born from a belief in hair as wellness

Anara was founded in 2009 by trichologist Dr. Kavitha Nair, who saw a gap between the clinical and the luxurious. Hair health, she believed, should be both scientifically rigorous and deeply sensorial.

The name Anara — meaning 'pomegranate' in Sanskrit — was chosen for its symbol of renewal, abundance, and inner vitality. Every treatment we offer carries that spirit.

Today, from our atelier in Alwarpet, we see clients from across Tamil Nadu and beyond, each seeking what only a true hair spa can provide: restoration at the root.

Our Approach

Science meets tradition

We combine trichological diagnostics with time-tested Ayurvedic formulations. Our specialists hold certifications in both cosmetology and integrative wellness, ensuring every recommendation is grounded in evidence.

We source our botanical ingredients from certified organic farms in Kerala and the Nilgiris. Cold-pressed, unrefined, and as close to their natural state as possible.

No shortcuts. No filler ingredients. No rushed appointments.

What We Stand For

Three commitments

Intention

Every appointment begins with a consultation. We listen before we touch. Your hair's story guides everything that follows.

Purity

We use only clean, botanical formulations. No sulphates, parabens, or synthetic fragrance ever enters our treatments.

Mastery

Our specialists train for a minimum of two years before treating clients independently. Excellence is non-negotiable.
